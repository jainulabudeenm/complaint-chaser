# Emails

## Voice

The user signs these emails, so they must sound like a calm, careful person and not a bot or a lawyer.

- Short sentences. One fact per line. Number the facts.
- Polite and firm. No insults, no anger, no "kindly do the needful", no "I am extremely disappointed".
- Zero em dashes. No AI-tell words (delve, unlock, elevate, seamless, leverage, game-changer,
  testament, "I hope this email finds you well").
- Every number comes from an invoice, an order mail or a calculation shown in the email.
- Quote their words exactly, in quotes, with the date and the name of who wrote them.
- Quote a law by its name and rule number, plus the few words that matter. Do not paste whole
  sections.
- 1 clear ask. Say the amount, where the money goes and what proof you want (a reference number).
- 1 deadline as a date, a time and a timezone. Not "within 48 hours".
- State the next step as a plain fact. Name only steps the user will really take.
- Plain text body. Sign with their name as the profile shows it.
- In a ticket thread, keep the ticket tag in the subject. Reply to the newest message from the
  company, and set `to` and `cc` yourself. Do not trust reply-all.

## 1. First ask

```
Hello,

<Order or invoice number>. <What I bought, when, what I paid.>

What went wrong:
1. <fact, with date>
2. <fact, with date>
3. <fact, with the evidence: "Photo 1 shows ...">

<The rule or their own words that apply, in 1 or 2 lines.>

Please <the ask: refund Rs. X to my original payment method / restore my price of Rs. X a year>.
Please send the refund reference number.

<If needed: "Please do not offer store credit. I want a refund to my card.">

If you cannot approve this, please send my case to a supervisor and give me the reason in writing.

Regards,
<Name>
```

## 2. Firm reply to a refusal, a stall or silence

```
Hi <agent name>,

Ticket <number>, Order <number>.

<1 line: what happened since the last mail. For example: "I reported this on 14 Nov, the day of delivery, and sent the photos the same day. I have had no reply since.">

<Answer their exact refusal in 1 or 2 lines, with the source. For example: "Your FAQ says you replace items with "misaligned cuts, poor adhesive". Photos 1 and 2 show a misaligned cut before I applied it.">

<The rule. For example: "Rule 7(4) of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 says an online store shall not "refuse to refund consideration, if paid", when goods are defective. The Rule makes no exception for skins.">

Please:
1. Refund the full Rs. <amount> to my original payment method, and send the reference number.
2. <Second ask, for example: share the name and contact of your Grievance Officer, as Rule 4(4) requires.>

If this is not resolved by <date, time, timezone>, I will file a complaint with <the next step>.

Regards,
<Name>
```

## 3. Accepting an offer (only after the user says "send")

Lock in every detail in writing, because the agent who promised may not be the one who pays.

```
Hi <agent name>,

Thank you for reviewing my case and for this decision.

I accept <the exact offer, with the amount>.

Please confirm 2 points in writing:
1. The full Rs. <amount> goes back to my original <card or payment method>, with no fee or deduction. Please send the refund reference number.
2. <What must not change. For example: "The refund does not change my domain registration. It stays registered until October 2032.">

<If a helpline complaint is open: "I will update the National Consumer Helpline (grievance <number>) when the refund reaches my card.">

Regards,
<Name>
```

## 4. Formal notice (only with a new yes from the user)

Send it to the address their Terms name for legal notices. Address the legal company, not the
brand. Attach the invoices and the support emails as PDFs.

```
Subject: Formal notice: <issue>, <account or order>, ticket <number>

To: <legal company name>, Legal Department

1. I am a consumer in <country>. I bought <what> on <date> (<invoice numbers>).
2. <Numbered facts, each with a date and an invoice or email.>
3. Your staff admitted the error in writing: "<quote>" (<name>, <date>).
4. Your support refused to fix it on <dates>.
5. <The law, with the rule number. Point out that their own Terms keep the user's legal rights, if they say so.>

I ask you to:
a. <ask 1, with the amount>
b. <ask 2>

Please also give me your registered postal address for a formal complaint.

If this is not done by <date: about 15 days out>, I will file a complaint with <consumer commission, with URL>, without further notice.

<Name>
<Date>
```

## 5. Helpline complaint text (the user files it)

Keep it under the form's character limit. Check the limit on the form.

```
<Company> (<website>) <did what> on <date>. Order/ticket <numbers>. I paid Rs. <amount> on <date>.
<2 to 4 numbered facts.>
The company refused on <date>, saying "<quote>".
<Rule that applies.>
I want: <the ask>.
```

List the files to attach: invoices, the email thread as a PDF, photos.
